## What Should Chinese Investors Know Before Buying Property in Türkiye?

Chinese nationals considering a Turkish property investment should obtain reliable information about the proposed transaction before signing binding documents or transferring funds.

Several issues deserve particular attention.

### 1. Property Ownership and Title Deed Verification

A buyer should establish who legally owns the property and whether any registered mortgages, restrictions, annotations or other issues may affect the acquisition.

The legal status of a property should be reviewed independently of marketing materials or representations made during a property viewing.

### 2. Property Eligibility for Turkish Citizenship

Under the applicable real estate investment route, a qualifying investment of at least USD 400,000 or its permitted equivalent is generally required, together with the applicable three-year restriction on resale and other legal conditions.

However, the advertised price or purchase price alone does not establish eligibility.

Ownership history, valuation, payment documentation, land registry requirements and the relevant conformity assessment must be considered.

The competent Turkish authorities make the final eligibility and citizenship determinations.

Foreign buyers should understand the banking and documentation requirements applicable to the transaction.

Where citizenship eligibility is intended, the relevant payment trail, foreign-exchange documentation and land registry records may be especially important.

## Turkish Citizenship by Investment: An Important Consideration for Property Buyers

Türkiye provides a citizenship by investment framework that includes qualifying real estate investments.

For investors considering the property route, the minimum qualifying amount is generally USD 400,000, subject to the applicable legal requirements.

An investment meeting the financial threshold does not automatically guarantee citizenship.

The legal status of the property, transaction documentation, relevant investment restrictions and the applicant's individual circumstances remain important.

Chinese investors who are considering purchasing one or more properties should seek an individual assessment before assuming that a proposed acquisition will satisfy the requirements.
