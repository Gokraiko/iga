## First Milestone: Establishing the Banking Structure

The case began with the opening of dedicated bank accounts at QNB Bank A.Ş.

The account-opening arrangements were carried out under a power of attorney.

Accounts in Turkish lira, US dollars and euros were established to support the investment transaction and related documentation.

This was an important preparatory stage because the banking records would subsequently form part of the investment conformity file.

The case demonstrates that authorized legal representation may assist with permitted banking procedures, subject to the bank's identification, compliance and representation requirements.

## Second Milestone: Documenting the $500,000 Investment

The case file records a capital transfer of USD 500,000.

The funds were converted into TRY 21,995,950, with the relevant foreign exchange purchase documentation obtained.

A three-year deposit restriction commitment was also established as part of the qualifying investment arrangement.

These records formed part of the documentary foundation for the subsequent investment conformity review.

The important distinction in this case was between transferring funds into a bank and establishing that the banking investment met the relevant citizenship requirements.

The latter required the appropriate records and confirmation from the competent authority.

## Third Milestone: BDDK Certificate of Conformity

After the banking investment documentation had been prepared, the relevant file proceeded to the Banking Regulation and Supervision Agency (BDDK).

The case documentation records that BDDK issued the Certificate of Conformity for the qualifying USD 500,000 bank deposit.

This was a significant milestone.

The certificate confirmed compliance with the applicable investment condition, allowing the application to proceed to the subsequent immigration and citizenship stages.

However, the Certificate of Conformity did not itself grant citizenship.

The final citizenship decision remained a separate administrative and legal determination.

## Fourth Milestone: Residence Permit Issuance

Following the investment conformity stage, the client obtained the relevant investment-related short-term residence permit.

The residence permit formed part of the administrative documentation supporting the citizenship application.

This stage illustrates why an investment file and a citizenship application should not be treated as identical procedures.

Investment conformity, residence status and citizenship approval are separate legal milestones.

## Fifth Milestone: Formal Citizenship Application

With the relevant investment, conformity and residence documentation available, the citizenship application was submitted to the Istanbul Provincial Directorate of Population and Citizenship Affairs.

The application file included the required records associated with the qualifying banking investment and the applicant's administrative status.

The case then proceeded through the competent authorities' citizenship review process.

The available case summary does not establish a precise processing timeline. Therefore, no specific duration is presented as typical or guaranteed.

## Final Milestone: Turkish Citizenship Approved

The application ultimately received final citizenship approval through the relevant presidential decision under Article 12 of Turkish Citizenship Law No. 5901.

The recorded outcome was the successful acquisition of Turkish citizenship.

The case therefore documents the progression of one bank deposit investment from the initial banking arrangements through conformity confirmation and the final citizenship decision.

It is an example of an approved application, not a promise that future applicants will receive the same result.

The documented file progressed through banking, investment conformity, immigration and citizenship procedures.
